As part of my semester project, I developed spreadsheet-based design calculations for both gravity and reinforced-concrete cantilever retaining walls. For each wall type, I evaluated two configurations: without a shear key and with a shear key.

The work included defining geometric, soil, concrete, and reinforcement parameters; calculating active earth pressure and lateral forces; determining overturning and resisting moments; and checking stability against overturning, sliding, and bearing-capacity failure. The spreadsheets were arranged so that wall dimensions and design assumptions could be adjusted and the resulting safety checks updated through formulas.

For the cantilever retaining wall, I also carried out the structural design of the stem, heel, and toe, including flexural and shear checks, required steel-area calculations, selection of reinforcement bar sizes and spacing, horizontal and vertical reinforcement, temperature and shrinkage reinforcement, and reinforcement curtailment.

The final design sheets summarize the adopted dimensions, reinforcement details, and whether the governing stability and shear checks are satisfied.
