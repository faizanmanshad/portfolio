Working as a Track Delivery Specialist at [OpenSpace](https://www.openspace.ai/), reviewing high-frequency 360° reality capture site data to verify construction progress and validate trade milestones across commercial building projects.

### Key Responsibilities & Workflow

- **Reality Capture Verification:** Review 360° photographic site walkthroughs, mapping captured field conditions against georeferenced floor plans, CAD sheets, and BIM coordinates.
- **Trade Milestone Tracking:** Assess and record installation progress across building components, including concrete works, masonry walls, interior partitions, MEP ductwork, and primary utility runs.
- **Quality Assurance & Verification:** Perform quality checks on tracking datasets within OpenSpace Track to ensure reported trade percentages accurately match visible site execution.
- **Field Condition Observation:** Assist project teams by identifying differences between planned design drawings and visible site progress to support clear progress reporting.
