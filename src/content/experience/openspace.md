<section class="scope-panel" aria-labelledby="key-responsibilities--workflow">
  <h2 id="key-responsibilities--workflow">Key Responsibilities &amp; Workflow</h2>
  <p>Working as a Track Delivery Specialist at <a href="https://www.openspace.ai/">OpenSpace</a>, reviewing high-frequency 360° reality capture site data to verify construction progress and validate trade milestones across commercial building projects.</p>
  <details>
    <summary>Reality Capture Verification</summary>
    <p>Review 360° photographic site walkthroughs, mapping captured field conditions against georeferenced floor plans, CAD sheets, and BIM coordinates.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Trade Milestone Tracking</summary>
    <p>Assess and record installation progress across building components, including concrete works, masonry walls, interior partitions, MEP ductwork, and primary utility runs.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Quality Assurance &amp; Verification</summary>
    <p>Perform quality checks on tracking datasets within OpenSpace Track to ensure reported trade percentages accurately match visible site execution.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Field Condition Observation</summary>
    <p>Assist project teams by identifying differences between planned design drawings and visible site progress to support clear progress reporting.</p>
  </details>
