As a Track Delivery Specialist at OpenSpace, I bridge physical construction execution and intelligent digital reporting by translating high-frequency 360° reality captures into structured, auditable progress metrics for major global building projects.

### Key Operational Responsibilities

- **360° Reality Capture Analysis:** Systematically review high-resolution 360° site walkthrough captures, cross-referencing panoramic imagery with georeferenced floor plans and BIM coordinates.
- **Multidisciplinary Component Verification:** Track the installation and completion status of critical trade components, including cast-in-place concrete, masonry blockwork, light-gauge framing, MEP ductwork, and primary utility runs.
- **Milestone Validation & Anomaly Detection:** Verify trade completion milestones against schedule baselines, flagging installation variances, unapproved field deviations, and site clashes before they escalate into costly rework.
- **Data Integrity & Quality Assurance:** Audit computer-vision and AI-tracked progress datasets, performing manual quality checks to ensure percent-complete figures delivered to general contractors and owners are completely reliable.

### Dual-Screen Production Workflow

My day-to-day workflow relies on an intensive dual-monitor operational setup:
1. **Primary Display (Reality Viewport):** Navigating 360-degree interactive walkthroughs, performing photogrammetric visual inspection of site assemblies at exact coordinate timestamps.
2. **Secondary Display (Verification & Coordination):** Cross-checking active floor plans, CAD/BIM overlays, sheet schedules, and the OpenSpace Track progress matrix to log verified quantities in real time.
