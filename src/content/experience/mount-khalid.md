Completed an 8-week engineering internship at **Mount Khalid** in Gulberg Greens, Islamabad, working under the supervision of the General Manager, Senior Project Manager, and Project Architect on a 45-story high-rise residential tower project.

### Key Responsibilities & Engineering Scope

- **High-Rise BIM Model Development:** Contributed to the 3D Building Information Model (BIM) of the 45-story residential tower, modeling structural framing, vertical cores, and floor plates.
- **Structural Design Coordination:** Assisted the engineering team in reviewing structural layouts, reinforcement schedules, and multi-story load-bearing assemblies.
- **Project Planning:** Participated in construction scheduling, workflow coordination, and progress tracking across office and field teams.
- **On-Site Construction Activities:** Conducted regular site inspections of heavy raft/slab reinforcement grids, tower crane operations, and vertical core starter bars to bridge digital BIM models with field execution.
