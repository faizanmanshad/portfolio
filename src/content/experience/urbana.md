<section class="scope-panel" aria-labelledby="key-responsibilities--drafting-scope">
  <h2 id="key-responsibilities--drafting-scope">Key Responsibilities &amp; Drafting Scope</h2>
  <p>Working as a remote Revit Drafter (20 hours per week) with <a href="https://urbana.au/">Urbana</a> (Australian-based company; <a href="https://urbana.au/quick-links/team/#Faizan-Manshad">Team Profile</a>), supporting modular public infrastructure projects across Australia, including prefabricated pedestrian footbridges, access boardwalks, and park structures.</p>
  <details>
    <summary>Parametric 3D BIM Modeling</summary>
    <p>Develop and update structural models in Autodesk Revit (LOD 300–350), detailing steel truss assemblies, timber/composite decking, balustrade systems, and foundation interfaces.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Drawing Set Production</summary>
    <p>Prepare coordinated drawing packages including preliminary layouts, General Arrangement (GA) drawings, foundation setting-out plans, and shop/fabrication details.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Review Comment Integration</summary>
    <p>Update drawing sheets in response to structural engineer reviews, architectural markups, and client requirements while maintaining clear drawing revisions.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Dimensional Coordination</summary>
    <p>Cross-check dimensions and joint details to facilitate clean off-site manufacturing and straightforward on-site assembly.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Australian Standards Alignment</summary>
    <p>Coordinate drafting details in accordance with relevant Australian Standards (such as AS 5100 bridge series, AS 4100 steel structures, and AS 1428 access provisions).</p>
  </details>
</section>
