Work part-time as a Revit Drafter (20hrs/week) supporting modular public-infrastructure projects, primarily pedestrian bridges. Develop and revise BIM models and prepare preliminary, general arrangement and fabrication/shop drawings, while incorporating engineering and design-review comments into updated drawing packages. Responsibilities also include reviewing reference information, checking dimensions and material data, resolving modelling and detailing issues, managing drawing revisions and documentation, and maintaining clear technical records and progress updates for the wider project team.
