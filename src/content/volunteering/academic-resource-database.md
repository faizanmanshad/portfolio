Kashan Shafiq started the FA22 archive so students could find course materials in one place. I digitised past exams and recorded study sessions with classmates. Haider Ali taught the rebar modelling sessions.

## What’s in the archive

The Google Drive repository is structured semester-by-semester across the undergraduate degree:

- **1st to 8th Semester Coursework:** Lecture slide decks, course outlines, lab testing manuals, and assignment problem sets.
- **Archived Past Examinations:** Midterm and final examination papers collected and organized to assist with revision.
- **Codes & Engineering Aids:** Standard structural reference codes (ACI 318, AISC 360, ASCE 7, UBC-97) and calculation sheets.
- **FYDP Documentation:** Departmental formatting guidelines and sample Final Year Design Project templates.

## Peer-Learning Playlists & Study Sessions

To support batchmates during complex engineering courses, we recorded study meetings and tutorial walkthroughs, sharing them as unlisted YouTube playlists with our class:

- **Quantity Estimation & Takeoff:** 7 walkthrough videos covering measurement takeoff methods, rate analysis, and assignment problem solving.
- **Matrix Structural Analysis:** 3 focused videos on stiffness matrix formulations and problem prep, collaborated with Kashan Shafiq.
- **Reinforced Concrete Design 2 (RCD 2):** 8 videos dedicated to beam, column, and slab design calculations prior to finals.
- **Revit Fundamentals & House Project:** Practical sessions introducing Revit basics, plus an 11-video hands-on series teaching a study group of 4–5 classmates to model a residential house from scratch.
- **Rebar Modeling:** Structural rebar detailing and placement sessions led and taught by classmate Haider Ali.
- **Team Collaboration (Royal Residency):** 4 recorded sessions demonstrating multi-user collaborative modeling on a shared project.
- **Primavera P6 Scheduling (CEP):** Step-by-step walkthrough addressing common roadblocks for the teacher-assigned Complex Engineering Problem.
