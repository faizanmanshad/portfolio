Conducted for Reinforced Concrete Design II, this project built dynamic calculation workbooks in Excel for mass gravity and reinforced-concrete cantilever retaining walls with and without shear keys.

### Key Engineering Scope

- **Lateral Earth Pressures:** Automated Rankine active pressure calculations considering 13 ft retained height, soil friction angle (32°), unit weight (130 pcf), and surface surcharge.
- **Equilibrium & Stability Checks:** Coded automated formulas verifying safety factors against overturning (FS ≥ 2.0), sliding (FS ≥ 1.5), and bearing eccentricity within middle thirds.
- **Shear Key Engagement:** Evaluated passive soil resistance enhancements from an embedded 1.5 ft × 2.0 ft shear key, increasing sliding stability margins.
- **Cantilever Structural Detailing:** Computed flexural and shear reinforcement for the stem (#7 @ 11" c/c), heel slab (#8 @ 14" c/c), toe slab, and vertical curtailment intervals.
