<section class="scope-panel" aria-labelledby="key-engineering-scope">
  <h2 id="key-engineering-scope">Key Engineering Scope</h2>
  <p>Conducted for Reinforced Concrete Design II, this project built dynamic calculation workbooks in Excel for mass gravity and reinforced-concrete cantilever retaining walls with and without shear keys.</p>
  <details>
    <summary>Lateral Earth Pressures</summary>
    <p>Automated Rankine active pressure calculations considering 13 ft retained height, soil friction angle (32°), unit weight (130 pcf), and surface surcharge.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Equilibrium &amp; Stability Checks</summary>
    <p>Coded automated formulas verifying safety factors against overturning (FS ≥ 2.0), sliding (FS ≥ 1.5), and bearing eccentricity within middle thirds.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Shear Key Engagement</summary>
    <p>Evaluated passive soil resistance enhancements from an embedded 1.5 ft × 2.0 ft shear key, increasing sliding stability margins.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Cantilever Structural Detailing</summary>
    <p>Computed flexural and shear reinforcement for the stem (#7 @ 11" c/c), heel slab (#8 @ 14" c/c), toe slab, and vertical curtailment intervals.</p>
  </details>
