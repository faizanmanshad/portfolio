<section class="scope-panel" aria-labelledby="key-engineering-scope">
  <h2 id="key-engineering-scope">Key Engineering Scope</h2>
  <p>This project determined optimal coagulation chemistry for raw surface water and developed the hydraulic design of a municipal-scale sedimentation-cum-coagulation treatment basin.</p>
  <details>
    <summary>Water Quality Testing</summary>
    <p>Tested raw samples for baseline turbidity, pH, total dissolved solids (TDS), total suspended solids (TSS), and electrical conductivity.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Jar Test Optimization</summary>
    <p>Carried out controlled jar testing across varying alum dosages to establish the optimum coagulation dose for maximum turbidity removal.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Sedimentation Basin Sizing</summary>
    <p>Dimensioned a rectangular continuous-flow settling tank, calculating detention time, surface overflow rates, and floor slope for sludge accumulation.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Hydraulic Controls</summary>
    <p>Sized inlet perforated baffle walls for uniform dispersion and effluent V-notch collection launders to satisfy standard weir loading rates.</p>
  </details>
</section>
