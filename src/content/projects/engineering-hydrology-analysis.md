This study conducted a complete hydrological evaluation of a 600 km² drainage basin, analyzing climate data, precipitation distribution, and catchment runoff response.

### Key Engineering Scope

- **Catchment Water Balance:** Formulated the hydrologic water budget equation (P = R + E + ΔS) to evaluate storage variation from 1,200 mm annual rainfall, 450 mm runoff, and 600 mm evapotranspiration losses.
- **Rain Gauge Network Adequacy:** Evaluated network sufficiency across 6 gauging stations using statistical coefficient of variation (Cv = 20%) against a 6.5% allowable estimation error margin.
- **Missing Data & Double-Mass Curve:** Reconstructed missing rainfall data using Arithmetic Mean and Normal Ratio methods, and verified record consistency via Double-Mass Curve analysis.
- **Spatial Mean Rainfall:** Calculated areal precipitation across the catchment using Thiessen Polygon and Isohyetal contour methods to estimate peak basin discharge.
