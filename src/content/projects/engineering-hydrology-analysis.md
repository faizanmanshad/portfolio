<section class="scope-panel" aria-labelledby="key-engineering-scope">
  <h2 id="key-engineering-scope">Key Engineering Scope</h2>
  <p>This study conducted a complete hydrological evaluation of a 600 km² drainage basin, analyzing climate data, precipitation distribution, and catchment runoff response.</p>
  <details>
    <summary>Catchment Water Balance</summary>
    <p>Formulated the hydrologic water budget equation (P = R + E + ΔS) to evaluate storage variation from 1,200 mm annual rainfall, 450 mm runoff, and 600 mm evapotranspiration losses.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Rain Gauge Network Adequacy</summary>
    <p>Evaluated network sufficiency across 6 gauging stations using statistical coefficient of variation (Cv = 20%) against a 6.5% allowable estimation error margin.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Missing Data &amp; Double-Mass Curve</summary>
    <p>Reconstructed missing rainfall data using Arithmetic Mean and Normal Ratio methods, and verified record consistency via Double-Mass Curve analysis.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Spatial Mean Rainfall</summary>
    <p>Calculated areal precipitation across the catchment using Thiessen Polygon and Isohyetal contour methods to estimate peak basin discharge.</p>
  </details>
</section>
