This Quantity Surveying project prepared complete Bill of Quantities (BOQ) sheets and cost estimations for a multi-storey building directly from structural and architectural drawings.

### Key Engineering Scope

- **Concrete Volume Takeoff:** Computed net wet and dry volumes across foundations (F1–F10), columns, grade beams, shear walls, retaining walls, floor slabs, and staircases.
- **Masonry & Architectural Finishes:** Quantified material requirements for brickwork, mortar, internal plastering, and cement finishes incorporating shrinkage and wastage allowances.
- **Steel Rebar Takeoff:** Formulated comprehensive Bar Bending Schedules for bar sizes #3 through #8 across slabs, beams, and columns, detailing cut lengths and splices.
- **Cost Consolidation:** Totaled steel requirements at approximately 111,854 kg (~112 metric tons), deriving final material cost estimates and tender summaries.
