Conducted for Project Planning and Management, this project formulated the full construction baseline schedule and resource distribution for a residential dwelling under a one-year deadline.

### Key Engineering Scope

- **WBS & OBS Hierarchy:** Structured a 4-level Work Breakdown Structure covering substructure, superstructure, masonry, MEP services, and finishing phases.
- **Activity Sequencing:** Scheduled over 60 discrete activities with Finish-to-Start (FS) and Start-to-Start (SS) dependencies, deriving durations from labor productivity norms.
- **CPM Network Analysis:** Identified critical path tasks, early/late milestone dates, and float tolerances in Oracle Primavera P6.
- **Resource Management:** Modeled labor allocation histograms, leveled peak trades to eliminate over-allocation, and generated planned cumulative progress S-curves.
