<section class="scope-panel" aria-labelledby="key-engineering-scope">
  <h2 id="key-engineering-scope">Key Engineering Scope</h2>
  <p>Conducted for Project Planning and Management, this project formulated the full construction baseline schedule and resource distribution for a residential dwelling under a one-year deadline.</p>
  <details>
    <summary>WBS &amp; OBS Hierarchy</summary>
    <p>Structured a 4-level Work Breakdown Structure covering substructure, superstructure, masonry, MEP services, and finishing phases.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Activity Sequencing</summary>
    <p>Scheduled over 60 discrete activities with Finish-to-Start (FS) and Start-to-Start (SS) dependencies, deriving durations from labor productivity norms.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>CPM Network Analysis</summary>
    <p>Identified critical path tasks, early/late milestone dates, and float tolerances in Oracle Primavera P6.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Resource Management</summary>
    <p>Modeled labor allocation histograms, leveled peak trades to eliminate over-allocation, and generated planned cumulative progress S-curves.</p>
  </details>
</section>
