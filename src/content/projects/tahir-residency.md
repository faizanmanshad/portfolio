<section class="scope-panel" aria-labelledby="tahir-scope">
  <h2 id="tahir-scope">Key Engineering Scope</h2>
  <p>This residential BIM project (Tahir Residency) executed end-to-end parametric modeling and technical documentation for a contemporary multi-level residential dwelling.</p>
  <details>
    <summary>Parametric Architectural Modeling</summary>
    <p>Developed architectural envelopes, internal layouts, curtain glazing, and custom families in Autodesk Revit.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Structural Coordination</summary>
    <p>Aligned reinforced concrete columns, beams, and slab systems in 3D to resolve spatial clashes prior to construction.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Solar Path & Insolation Studies</summary>
    <p>Performed dynamic solar trajectory simulations in Insight 360 to optimize passive daylighting and shading louvers.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Drawing Documentation</summary>
    <p>Generated tagged floor plans, building elevations, callout wall sections, and itemized material takeoff schedules.</p>
  </details>
</section>
