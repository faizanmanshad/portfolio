This project developed a comprehensive architectural design and BIM documentation package for a 40' x 80' residential plot in Islamabad, strictly adhering to Capital Development Authority (CDA) zoning regulations.

### Key Engineering Scope

- **CDA By-Law Compliance:** Implemented mandatory front, rear, and side setbacks, floor area ratios (FAR), and allowable covered area restrictions.
- **Parametric 3D BIM Model:** Built the virtual structure in Autodesk Revit, developing detailed walls, floor slabs, custom staircases, and window schedules.
- **Architectural Documentation:** Extracted coordinated drawing sets including dimensioned floor plans, elevations, wall sections, and room area schedules.
- **Spatial Optimization:** Organized functional family living zones, master bedrooms, cross-ventilation light wells, and covered car porch spaces.
