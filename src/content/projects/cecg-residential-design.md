  <h2 id="key-engineering-scope">Key Engineering Scope</h2>
  <p>This project developed a comprehensive architectural design and BIM documentation package for a 40' x 80' residential plot in Islamabad, strictly adhering to Capital Development Authority (CDA) zoning regulations.</p>
  <details>
    <summary>CDA By-Law Compliance</summary>
    <p>Implemented mandatory front, rear, and side setbacks, floor area ratios (FAR), and allowable covered area restrictions.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Parametric 3D BIM Model</summary>
    <p>Built the virtual structure in Autodesk Revit, developing detailed walls, floor slabs, custom staircases, and window schedules.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Architectural Documentation</summary>
    <p>Extracted coordinated drawing sets including dimensioned floor plans, elevations, wall sections, and room area schedules.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Spatial Optimization</summary>
    <p>Organized functional family living zones, master bedrooms, cross-ventilation light wells, and covered car porch spaces.</p>
  </details>
</section>
