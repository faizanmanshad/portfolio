Conducted for the Design of Structures course, this project delivered a complete 3D structural analysis and foundation design for the NUST Faisal Masjid reinforced concrete frame.

### Key Engineering Scope

- **3D Frame Modeling:** Built the full multi-level structural geometry in ETABS, capturing high-ceiling prayer halls, open central spans, and perimeter columns.
- **Seismic & Gravity Analysis:** Applied gravity and lateral load combinations per ASCE 7-16 and BCP-2021 for Seismic Zone 2B, performing Equivalent Static and Response Spectrum checks.
- **Frame Member Sizing:** Designed reinforced concrete columns and moment-resisting beams to satisfy ultimate flexure, shear limits, and drift criteria.
- **Foundation Design in SAFE:** Exported support reactions to CSI SAFE for slab and footing sizing, punching shear verification, and soil pressure checks.
