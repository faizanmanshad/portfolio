  <h2 id="key-engineering-scope">Key Engineering Scope</h2>
  <p>Conducted for the Design of Structures course, this project delivered a complete 3D structural analysis and foundation design for the NUST Mosque reinforced concrete frame.</p>
  <details>
    <summary>3D Frame Modeling</summary>
    <p>Built the full multi-level structural geometry in ETABS, capturing high-ceiling prayer halls, open central spans, and perimeter columns.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Seismic &amp; Gravity Analysis</summary>
    <p>Applied gravity and lateral load combinations per ASCE 7-16 and BCP-2021 for Seismic Zone 2B, performing Equivalent Static and Response Spectrum checks.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Frame Member Sizing</summary>
    <p>Designed reinforced concrete columns and moment-resisting beams to satisfy ultimate flexure, shear limits, and drift criteria.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Foundation Design in SAFE</summary>
    <p>Exported support reactions to CSI SAFE for slab and footing sizing, punching shear verification, and soil pressure checks.</p>
  </details>
</section>
