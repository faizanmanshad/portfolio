<section class="scope-panel" aria-labelledby="key-engineering-scope">
  <h2 id="key-engineering-scope">Key Engineering Scope</h2>
  <p>Conducted for Indeterminate Structural Analysis, this project compared classical manual structural solution techniques against numerical finite-element modeling in RISA-2D for indeterminate frames.</p>
  <details>
    <summary>Classical Hand Calculations</summary>
    <p>Solved indeterminate reactions and member end moments utilizing Moment Distribution and Slope-Deflection methods under point and distributed loads.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>RISA-2D Computer Modeling</summary>
    <p>Assembled the computational frame model with boundary constraints and load combinations to extract reactions, nodal displacements, and internal shears.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Force Diagram Verification</summary>
    <p>Compared Axial Force Diagrams (AFD), Shear Force Diagrams (SFD), and Bending Moment Diagrams (BMD) across both methods.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Discrepancy &amp; Convergence Review</summary>
    <p>Analyzed minor variations between techniques, identifying effects of member axial deformations and computational discretization.</p>
  </details>
</section>
