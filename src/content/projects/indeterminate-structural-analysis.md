Conducted for Indeterminate Structural Analysis, this project compared classical manual structural solution techniques against numerical finite-element modeling in RISA-2D for indeterminate frames.

### Key Engineering Scope

- **Classical Hand Calculations:** Solved indeterminate reactions and member end moments utilizing Moment Distribution and Slope-Deflection methods under point and distributed loads.
- **RISA-2D Computer Modeling:** Assembled the computational frame model with boundary constraints and load combinations to extract reactions, nodal displacements, and internal shears.
- **Force Diagram Verification:** Compared Axial Force Diagrams (AFD), Shear Force Diagrams (SFD), and Bending Moment Diagrams (BMD) across both methods.
- **Discrepancy & Convergence Review:** Analyzed minor variations between techniques, identifying effects of member axial deformations and computational discretization.
