<section class="scope-panel" aria-labelledby="key-engineering-scope">
  <h2 id="key-engineering-scope">Key Engineering Scope</h2>
  <p>This residential BIM project (Haroon Residency) entailed multi-disciplinary digital modeling and technical documentation for a modern multi-level residential building.</p>
  <details>
    <summary>Integrated 3D BIM Model</summary>
    <p>Built synchronized architectural and structural assemblies in Autodesk Revit encompassing structural concrete framing and masonry partitions.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Solar Exposure Analysis</summary>
    <p>Performed seasonal daylight studies to evaluate room insolation and position exterior shading projections to mitigate heat gain.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Coordinated Drawing Packages</summary>
    <p>Produced dimensioned foundation plans, structural framing layouts, longitudinal sections, and exterior facade elevations.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Automated Schedules</summary>
    <p>Extracted window and door schedules, floor area measurements, and material quantities directly from the parametric BIM database.</p>
  </details>
</section>
