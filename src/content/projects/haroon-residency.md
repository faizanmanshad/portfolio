Haroon Residency entailed multi-disciplinary digital modeling and technical documentation for a high-end residential project.

### Key Engineering Scope

- **Integrated 3D BIM Model:** Built synchronized architectural and structural assemblies in Autodesk Revit encompassing structural concrete framing and masonry partitions.
- **Solar Exposure Analysis:** Performed seasonal daylight studies to evaluate room insolation and position exterior shading projections to mitigate heat gain.
- **Coordinated Drawing Packages:** Produced dimensioned foundation plans, structural framing layouts, longitudinal sections, and exterior facade elevations.
- **Automated Schedules:** Extracted window and door schedules, floor area measurements, and material quantities directly from the parametric BIM database.
