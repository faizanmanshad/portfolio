<section class="scope-panel" aria-labelledby="key-engineering-scope">
  <h2 id="key-engineering-scope">Key Engineering Scope</h2>
  <p>Conducted for Properties of Concrete, this study evaluated the structural and physical performance of concrete produced with 50% recycled coarse aggregates (RCA) as a sustainable aggregate alternative.</p>
  <details>
    <summary>Mix Proportioning</summary>
    <p>Cast control batches with 100% natural coarse aggregates alongside test batches utilizing 50% recycled crushed concrete aggregates.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Workability Testing</summary>
    <p>Evaluated fresh concrete workability via standard slump cone tests, documenting increased paste water demand from porous aggregate mortar.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Compressive Strength Tests</summary>
    <p>Tested 7-day and 28-day cylinder compressive strengths on a Universal Testing Machine, mapping strength variations against control mixes.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Non-Destructive UPV Testing</summary>
    <p>Conducted Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity measurements per ASTM C597 to inspect internal density, voids, and relative structural homogeneity.</p>
  </details>
</section>
