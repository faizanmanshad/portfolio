Conducted for Properties of Concrete, this study evaluated the structural and physical performance of concrete produced with 50% recycled coarse aggregates (RCA) as a sustainable aggregate alternative.

### Key Engineering Scope

- **Mix Proportioning:** Cast control batches with 100% natural coarse aggregates alongside test batches utilizing 50% recycled crushed concrete aggregates.
- **Workability Testing:** Evaluated fresh concrete workability via standard slump cone tests, documenting increased paste water demand from porous aggregate mortar.
- **Compressive Strength Tests:** Tested 7-day and 28-day cylinder compressive strengths on a Universal Testing Machine, mapping strength variations against control mixes.
- **Non-Destructive UPV Testing:** Conducted Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity measurements per ASTM C597 to inspect internal density, voids, and relative structural homogeneity.
