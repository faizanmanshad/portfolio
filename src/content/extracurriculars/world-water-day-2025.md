Participated in the Video Competition organized on **22 March 2025** in connection with UN-Water World Water Day 2025 (*Glacier Preservation*), contributing to the cause of glacier preservation and water awareness. Hosted by the UNESCO Chair on Knowledge Systems for Integrated Water Resources Management at COMSATS University Islamabad (CUI), Wah Campus, in association with the UNESCO Intergovernmental Hydrological Programme (IHP), UNESCO Pakistan National Commission, and the Pakistan Council of Research in Water Resources (PCRWR).
