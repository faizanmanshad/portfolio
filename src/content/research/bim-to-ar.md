This study originated from my BS Civil Engineering Final Year Project (FYP) at COMSATS University Islamabad, Wah Campus. We exported our Autodesk Revit architectural model as FBX and built three Android prototypes in Unity: one anchored to a printed image marker (Vuforia Engine), one placed on a detected ground plane (Google ARCore), and one positioned with geographic coordinates (Cesium for Unity), complete with custom C# interaction controls.

We tested all three prototypes on physical Android devices to compare practical model placement workflows on site. Those physical tests demonstrated live model placement behavior, though they did not establish a numerical setting-out tolerance for construction surveying.
