We exported our Revit model as FBX and built three Android prototypes in Unity. One used a printed marker, one placed the model on a detected ground plane, and one used geographic coordinates. We used Vuforia, ARCore and Cesium for Unity, with custom controls and AI-assisted C# scripting.

We tested all three prototypes on physical devices. Those tests demonstrated model placement. They did not establish a numerical tolerance for construction setting out.

Hassan Ashraf, Faizan Manshad, Kashan Shafiq and Faiz Fareed are the authors of the SSRN preprint. I am the corresponding author.
